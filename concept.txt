Project Name: AI Waste Segregation Assistant  
Why I Chose This Problem
Improper waste segregation is a common problem because people often do not know which category a waste item belongs to. This causes different types of waste to be mixed together, making recycling and proper waste management difficult.
Solution
The AI Waste Segregation Assistant allows users to upload an image of a waste item. AI identifies the item, classifies it as Plastic, Paper, Glass, Metal, Organic, or Other, and recommends the appropriate disposal method.
Who Will Benefit
This project will benefit households, students, schools, colleges, and environmentally conscious people by helping them understand how to properly segregate and dispose of waste.
